Privacy policy

Badnotes Notebook for iPad

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy describes the iPad app published by Pyie Sone Khaing Min. You can use its local note-taking features without an account. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Your notes stay on your device

Notebooks, handwriting, images, imported documents and settings are stored in the app's local database and files on your iPad. The publisher does not receive a copy of your notes through normal local use. Image cropping and background removal run on your device.

Apple's device backup and storage services may include app data depending on your device settings. Exports you save to Files or share to another app are handled by the destination you choose.

Optional Google Drive features

When Google sign-in is available in your installed build, connecting it is optional. The app uses your Google account identity to show the connected account and requests the drive.file permission to work with files it creates or that you explicitly make available to it. This is not permission to read your entire Drive.

If you enable backup or sync, selected notes, attachments and settings are transferred directly between the app and Google Drive. The iPad sign-in session is managed on your device through Google's native sign-in SDK. The publisher does not receive your Google password or the contents of these Drive backups.

Google data is used to provide the backup and sync features you choose. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google in the app and revoke access in your Google account permissions. Previously created backups remain in your Drive until you delete them there.

Linking devices and sharing

If you choose to link devices, the app sends note content and updates to the devices you approve over an encrypted connection. Only link devices you trust. The initial iPad store build does not configure an internet relay service. Sharing or exporting a file gives a copy to the recipient or service you choose.

Permissions and network requests

The app may ask for access to photos, the camera or the microphone when a feature needs it. You can manage these permissions in iPad Settings. Normal handwriting does not need these permissions.

Features that fetch external resources, including the first download of text-recognition resources, may contact their hosting services. Those services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested resource. Text recognition processes your document on the device; the app does not upload the document for this recognition.

Keeping and deleting information

Local app data stays on your device until you remove it through the app or delete the app. Back up notes before deleting the app. Deleting Badnotes does not delete exported files, backups stored elsewhere or copies shared with other people.

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use them to respond to your request and retain them as needed to resolve and follow up on it. Do not send passwords or notes that support does not need.

This website

These support pages do not use analytics or advertising scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles ordinary hosting logs under its privacy statement.
