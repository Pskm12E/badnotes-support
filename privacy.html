Privacy policy

Badnotes Notebook for iPad

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy describes the iPad app published by Pyie Sone Khaing Min. You can use its local note-taking features without an account. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Your notes stay on your device

Notebooks, handwriting, images, imported documents and settings are stored in the app's local database and files on your iPad. The publisher does not receive a copy of your notes through normal local use. Image cropping and background removal run on your device.

Apple's device backup and storage services may include app data depending on your device settings. Exports you save to Files or share to another app are handled by the destination you choose.

No cloud account required

Version 1.0.27 works without Google Drive sign-in or cloud sync. From version 1.0.28 onward, setup lets you choose to keep notes only on this device or also back up and sync through your own Google Drive. Local-only is the default. Notes are not uploaded to Drive unless you choose that option and authorize Google access.

Optional Google Drive backup uses storage in the Google account you connect. Badnotes requests access to the files it creates and basic account information to display the connected account. Sign-in credentials and tokens are handled by Google sign-in and device storage, not by a publisher-operated sign-in server. Google processes account and service information under its privacy policy. You can turn sync off or disconnect in Settings → Cloud & Backup. Disconnecting keeps local notes and does not delete existing Drive backups. Saved notebook changes sync when the devices are online; this does not stream handwriting live.

When you enable Drive backup, your saved notebook content, including inserted photos or videos, and the app's device identifiers used to coordinate sync are stored in your Google Drive. Google Sign-In may retain your account identifier to record your authorization and process your IP address, including an approximate location inferred from it, for fraud prevention. These functions support authentication, security and your chosen backups; Badnotes does not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Version 1.0.56 also offers optional Microsoft OneDrive backup and saved-notebook sync. Each user configures their own Microsoft application registration and authorizes their own Microsoft account. Badnotes requests access to its OneDrive app folder and basic account information; Microsoft handles sign-in and stores the notes and attachments you choose to sync under its privacy statement. This uses your OneDrive storage. Authentication tokens remain in device storage; the publisher does not operate a OneDrive authentication or note-storage server. Disconnecting leaves local notes and existing OneDrive backups in place.

Linking devices and sharing

If you choose to link devices, the app sends note content and updates to the devices you approve over an encrypted connection. Only link devices you trust. You can forget approved devices in Settings → Linked devices. Forgetting a device does not delete copies already shared with it. There is no publisher-operated internet relay in this release. For live linking over different networks, users can configure their own compatible relay. That service forwards encrypted traffic and handles connection information, including device IP addresses, under its operator's policies. Optional Google Drive or OneDrive sync transfers saved notebooks independently of live linking.

Sharing or exporting a file gives a copy to the recipient or service you choose.

Permissions and network requests

The app may ask for access to photos, the camera or the microphone when a feature needs it. You can manage these permissions in iPad Settings. Normal handwriting does not need these permissions.

Features that fetch external resources, including the first download of text-recognition resources, may contact their hosting services. Those services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested resource. Text recognition processes your document on the device; the app does not upload the document for this recognition.

Optional website browser, handwriting and fonts

The in-app browser loads websites you select, including search and AI websites. Those sites receive your requests and any information you enter or upload, and may store their own cookies and signed-in sessions. Their terms and privacy policies apply. Badnotes does not automatically send your notebooks to a website, and the publisher does not collect your browsing history. Some website sign-ins require Apple's separate secure browser screen; its session is separate from the movable browser panel.

Optional handwriting-to-text recognition runs on the device using Apple's Vision framework or local recognition resources. The user can review and edit recognized text before inserting it. Bundled fonts are included in the app. Custom fonts you import are stored locally and may be included with notebook backups or transfers when used in a note; respect the font owner's licence.

Keeping and deleting information

Local app data stays on your device until you remove it through the app or delete the app. Back up notes before deleting the app. Deleting Badnotes does not delete exported files, backups stored elsewhere or copies shared with other people.

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use them to respond to your request and retain them as needed to resolve and follow up on it. Do not send passwords or notes that support does not need.

This website

These support pages do not use analytics or advertising scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles ordinary hosting logs under its privacy statement.
