Write without Wi-Fi

Your notebooks are stored on your iPad. Writing, drawing and opening locally stored notes do not require a Google account or an internet connection. Cloud features and downloading additional resources need internet access.

Keep a copy of your notes

Open Settings → Cloud & Backup to create a local database recovery backup. Save it in Files and keep a second copy somewhere safe. You can also export individual notebooks.

Google Drive is the easier optional cloud setup. OneDrive is an advanced alternative requiring your own Microsoft application registration; follow the separate in-app guide. Both use your own account's storage. Live device linking is configured separately under Settings → Linked devices; internet linking requires your own compatible relay.

Deleting the app can remove its local notes. Export or back up anything you want to keep before deleting it. Backups you saved outside Badnotes remain separate from the app.

Move and zoom the page

Pinch with two fingers to zoom. With Apple Pencil input active, use a finger to move around the page. In version 1.0.56, double-tap with a navigating finger to fit the page inside the note area, including split view. The hand tool also lets you pan without drawing.

Optional writing setup and websites

Choose your default paper, pen and toolbar preferences during first-run setup, or choose Skip setup and change them later in Settings. Handwriting-to-text and custom fonts are optional.

The optional browser opens the actual websites you choose beside your notes. Website sign-in and conversation history are controlled by that provider. Some sign-ins need Open secure browser; signing in there does not transfer that session into the movable panel.
