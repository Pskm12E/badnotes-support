Write without Wi-Fi

Your notebooks are stored on your iPad. Writing, drawing and opening locally stored notes do not require a Google account or an internet connection. Cloud features and downloading additional resources need internet access.

Keep a copy of your notes

Open Settings → Cloud & Backup to create a local database backup. Save the backup in Files and keep a second copy somewhere safe. You can also export individual notebooks from the app.

Deleting the app can remove its local notes. Export or back up anything you want to keep before deleting it. Backups you saved outside Badnotes remain separate from the app.

Move and zoom the page

Pinch with two fingers to zoom. With Apple Pencil input active, use a finger to move around the page. The hand tool also lets you pan without drawing.

Google Drive

Google Drive is optional. Availability depends on the installed build. If sign-in is unavailable, local notebooks and local database backups remain available.
